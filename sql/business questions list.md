# ❓ Business Questions

The analysis is structured around the following business questions.

## Sales Performance

1. What is the total revenue and total units sold?
2. Which brands generate the highest revenue?
3. Which 10 products have the highest sales?
4. What are the monthly sales trends?

## Product & Pricing

5. Which brands have the highest average selling price?
6. Which price tier generates the most revenue?
7. What is the return rate by brand?
8. Which products have high sales volume but high return rates?
9. What percentage of revenue comes from the top 10 products?

## Inventory

10. Which products are potentially overstocked?
11. Which fast-moving products have insufficient current stock?

## Supplier & Replenishment

12. Which supplier has the greatest lead-time variability?
13. What is the expected demand during supplier lead time?
14. Which products may run out before supplier replenishment?
15. Which SKUs should the business prioritise for replenishment?
